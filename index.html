The marketplace

Where the deal happens, safely

1. List

Creators list rates, slots and services. Businesses list briefs and products.

2. Book

A business books a creator. The budget is funded into escrow the moment the deal is accepted.

3. Release

Delivery is confirmed, escrow releases to the creator instantly, both sides get a clean record.
